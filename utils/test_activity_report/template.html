Tests that ran

One row per pipeline: the Jenkins folder and job name, summed over all releases it ran in. The table has no overall rank. It opens sorted by estimated total cost, and each column sorts. Click a row for its issues, its runs with Argus links, and its owner. The Release filter limits every count, the cost, the hours and the issues to the runs of that release.

"Failed runs looked at" shows how many failed runs of the test a person triaged in Argus. A failed run counts as looked at when its Argus investigation status is "investigated", "in progress" or "ignored", or when an issue is linked to it. A low share means that nobody triaged the failures. The issue counts of such a test can then be too low, because a failure with no triage has no linked issue. "–" means that the test had no failed run.
